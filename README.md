# CAPRO Website

Website of **CAPRO, the Center for AI Innovation & Product Development**, at
Manipal Institute of Technology (MIT) Bengaluru.

> *To build impactful software solutions while creating experiential learning opportunities.*

**Live site:** (https://caprowebsite.vercel.app)

---

## About

CAPRO builds software for the institute and for industry use-cases, while giving students
hands-on experience in the full product development life cycle. All content on this site
comes from the CAPRO presentation. No metrics, partnerships or achievements appear until
they are confirmed.
